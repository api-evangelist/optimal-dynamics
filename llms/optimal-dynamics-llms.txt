# Optimal Dynamics

> Optimal Dynamics is the Decision Company for trucking. Its Transportation Decision System (TDS), CORE.ai, applies Artificial Decision Intelligence — born from 40 years of research at Princeton University — to automate and optimize strategic, tactical, and real-time planning for enterprise truckload carriers, including dispatch management, network management, bid analysis, and network simulation.
